# English Mint Sprint 42 — Meetings & Presentations Pilot

## Implemented
- Professional English Hub > Meetings & presentations pilot.
- Four original offline workplace communication questions: opening an agenda, polite clarification, introducing a presentation, and discussing preliminary results responsibly.
- Two categories (Meeting and Presentation), each with two exercises. Three answer options per exercise, explanatory feedback, optional speaking or writing prompts, category switching and next-question navigation.
- Pure model tests for distinct IDs, correct-answer indices, explanations and category distribution.
- No recording, live AI, paid API, official proficiency scoring, certificate, XP or Mint Bloom from previews.

## Honest scope
This is an initial pilot, **not** a comprehensive multi-turn meeting simulator, presentation recorder, workplace scoring rubric or approved professional curriculum. No third-party meeting data is accessed and no personal presentation content is stored.
Original Sprints 39–48 remain subject to the full SRS and independent editorial/device QA.
